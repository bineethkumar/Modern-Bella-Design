# Modern Bella Design: design brief

**Design read:** homeowners in the Maryland and DC area planning a kitchen or bath. They want to feel the quality of the cabinetry before they ever touch a door, and then buy with the ease of a modern store.

**Concept spine:** "After hours in the showroom." The site opens in a darkened kitchen lit only by a gold under-cabinet strip, and the visitor's scroll walks them up to one perfect door. Everything after the film is the showroom with the lights on: warm paper, black ink, gold hairlines.

**Delivery tier:** cinema (the scroll-scrubbed film carries the hero); the rest of the page uses CSS-only micro-motion.

**Locked palette** (taken from the client's logo, which overrides the palette bans):
- Ink `#0E0E0F`: script wordmark black, film shadows
- Paper `#F6F3EE`: the showroom with the lights on
- Bone `#FFFFFF`: cards
- Gold `#B8914F` with highlight `#D9B97A`: the DESIGN rule and hairlines in the logo
- Navy `#1D2A3A`: the Emerald Blue door finish, used as a secondary accent
- Stone `#6E6A63`: muted text. Line `#E4DED3`: hairlines

**Locked type:** Cormorant Garamond (display, italic accents) with Jost (UI, spaced caps). The serif is justified by the client's script logotype; Jost mirrors the letterspaced geometric "DESIGN" in the logo.

**Animation mode: animated-website**
- **Journey shape:** single-shot (one 12s film, Kling 3.0 Pro, start and end keyframes).
- **Journey:** wide establishing shot of the dark kitchen, a glide past the marble island, then settling on a navy shaker door with a champagne-gold pull. Chapter copy: "Cabinetry, after hours." The journey enacts the spine: walking into the showroom before it opens.
- **World grammar:** low-key, ink black shadows, warm gold practical light, satin paint, marble, locked exposure, centered subject.
- **Mobile framing:** subject centered; 720p mobile encode.
- **Delivery budget:** 32 MiB desktop, 16 MiB mobile.

**Section plan:** 1 journey hero (full-bleed film) · 2 finish studio (split: swatch rail plus real kitchen photography) · 3 collections (bento grid) · 4 process (numbered horizontal timeline with install rates) · 5 services (two-column index list) · 6 consultation (form on ink) · footer (with Management login bottom right only).

**Asset plan:** hero film plus exact-frame posters; client logo (ink and light cutouts); favicon from the script M; the supplier's real kitchen photography, door swatches and cabinet renders for all content imagery; launch cover.

**CTA inventory:**
- "Shop the collection": gold-leaf fill with a light sweep on hover
- "Book a design consult": hairline underline that draws in
- "Add to cart": ink square that flips to a check
- "Place order": full-width ink bar with a gold progress edge
- "Track an order": circle-arrow link
